# VentureFoundry OS™ — Governance, Risk and Compliance Baseline

## 1. Objective

This baseline prevents a startup or internal venture from treating governance as a late-stage administrative exercise. Controls are applied proportionally: lightweight at concept stage, stronger as customer, financial, workforce, data and operational exposure increases.

## 2. Governance model

### 2.1 Decision levels

| Level | Typical decisions | Authority |
|---|---|---|
| Venture team | experiments, backlog, minor spend within budget | Venture Owner and delegated leads |
| Gate review | pass, hold, return, pivot or stop | Sponsor-chaired gate panel |
| Portfolio review | priority, shared resources, investment envelope | Portfolio Steering Committee |
| Executive/Board | material capital, legal entity, major partnership, critical exposure | Authorized executive body |

### 2.2 Non-delegable accountabilities

- lawful and ethical operation;
- truthful reporting and evidence integrity;
- protection of people, customers and confidential information;
- solvency and controlled use of funds;
- critical incident escalation;
- approval of material commitments and external representations.

## 3. Required governance records

Every active venture maintains:

- approved charter and current gate;
- role and delegation matrix;
- decision log;
- risk and opportunity register;
- obligations register;
- budget, commitments and forecast;
- evidence repository;
- issue, incident and corrective-action log;
- stakeholder and communications register;
- change history and version status.

## 4. Risk taxonomy

Assess at minimum:

1. strategy and market;
2. customer and product;
3. finance and liquidity;
4. legal, regulatory and contractual;
5. information security, privacy and AI;
6. technology, architecture and reliability;
7. operations, suppliers and continuity;
8. people, competence, health and safety;
9. quality and customer outcomes;
10. ethics, inclusion, environment and reputation;
11. fraud, corruption and conflicts of interest;
12. intellectual property and third-party rights.

## 5. Risk assessment

Use a five-level likelihood and impact scale.

```text
Inherent Risk = Likelihood × Impact
Residual Risk = Remaining likelihood × Remaining impact after controls
```

Impact must consider financial loss, legal exposure, human harm, customer impact, confidentiality, service interruption and reputation.

### Escalation

| Residual rating | Required response |
|---:|---|
| 1–4 | manage within team |
| 5–9 | owner and treatment due date required |
| 10–15 | sponsor review and monitored treatment |
| 16–20 | gate hold unless formally accepted by authorized executive |
| 21–25 | stop activity and escalate immediately |

## 6. Compliance-by-stage

### Concept and discovery

- confidentiality and intellectual-property boundaries;
- lawful basis for research and data collection;
- consent where required;
- truthful public claims;
- conflict-of-interest disclosure.

### Validation and pilot

- pilot terms and responsibilities;
- privacy and security controls;
- customer or beneficiary protection;
- financial authorization;
- supplier due diligence;
- records retention.

### MVP and traction

- customer terms, service levels and support;
- secure development and access control;
- accounting and tax treatment;
- employment or contractor compliance;
- incident response;
- insurance and liability review where applicable.

### Scale and institutionalization

- formal process ownership;
- internal controls and assurance;
- business continuity and disaster recovery;
- vendor and concentration-risk management;
- regulatory horizon scanning;
- management review and corrective action;
- independent audit where exposure warrants it.

## 7. AI governance baseline

AI-supported work must be classified as:

- **assistive:** drafting, summarization, formatting and low-risk analysis;
- **advisory:** recommendations influencing a human decision;
- **automated:** execution of a defined action within approved limits;
- **restricted:** high-impact actions requiring explicit human approval or prohibited automation.

Minimum controls:

- approved use case and accountable owner;
- input-data classification;
- source verification for material claims;
- human validation proportional to impact;
- access control and secrets protection;
- logging and reproducibility where feasible;
- bias, safety and misuse review;
- fallback and manual override;
- prohibition on fabricating customer evidence, approvals or audit records.

## 8. Financial governance

Every venture must report:

- approved budget;
- actual spend;
- committed but unpaid spend;
- forecast to completion;
- available cash or funding;
- runway;
- unit economics or impact economics;
- unapproved exposure;
- revenue quality and collection status.

Material variance thresholds must be set before spending. A default escalation threshold is the lower of 10% of approved budget or an amount designated by the sponsor.

## 9. Intellectual-property controls

- identify background IP and new IP;
- record authorship and ownership;
- use licensed or permissioned third-party assets;
- review open-source licences before integration;
- define employee, contractor and partner assignment terms;
- control publication and disclosure;
- preserve invention, design and brand evidence;
- perform freedom-to-operate review when material.

## 10. Security and privacy baseline

At MVP stage or earlier when sensitive data is involved:

- least-privilege access;
- multi-factor authentication for privileged accounts;
- approved secrets handling;
- encrypted transport and appropriate storage protection;
- data minimization and retention rules;
- backup and recovery test;
- vulnerability and dependency management;
- incident reporting path;
- privacy notice and data-subject handling where applicable.

## 11. Assurance model

Use three lines proportionally:

1. **Delivery ownership:** teams operate controls and maintain evidence.
2. **Oversight:** finance, risk, legal, security, quality or equivalent functions challenge and monitor.
3. **Independent assurance:** internal audit, external audit or specialist review for material exposure.

## 12. Gate-review agenda

1. Decision requested.
2. Evidence and confidence.
3. Financial position and forecast.
4. Customer or impact outcomes.
5. Material risks, incidents and compliance status.
6. Capability and capacity.
7. Dependencies and assumptions.
8. Conditions, owner and deadline.
9. Recorded decision: pass, conditional pass, hold, return, pivot, stop or scale.

## 13. Records and confidentiality

Venture records are classified, versioned, access-controlled and retained according to legal, contractual and business requirements. Public examples must exclude client-confidential, personal, security-sensitive and proprietary operational details.
